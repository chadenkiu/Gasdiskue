Our Narrative

The Vision

Born from a university project, GasdiskueLips was conceived to solve a simple problem: the uncertainty of online shade selection. By integrating sophisticated WebAR textures with a curated palette of ethereal colors, we've created a boutique experience that lives entirely in your browser.

Every formula we "digitize" reflects our commitment to effortless elegance, ensuring that what you see on screen is exactly what you feel on your lips.
